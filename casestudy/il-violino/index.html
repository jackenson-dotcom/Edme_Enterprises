01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

A neighborhood story worth discovering.

Il Violino is an established Italian trattoria on Manhattan's Upper West Side. The opportunity was to translate its in-person character into a clearer digital identity—and connect the restaurant to the occasions, conversations, and people in its neighborhood.

The approach combines consistent restaurant storytelling, short-form content, local discovery, campaign development, and measurement of actions that can signal customer interest.

Strategy + hands-on execution

03 / THE WORK

From idea to execution.

01 / POSITIONING

Brand consistency

Trattoria-first messaging, a warm burgundy-and-cream visual direction, and clearer restaurant storytelling across marketing touchpoints.

02 / CONTENT

Instagram & creative

Content calendars, captions, Reel development, original restaurant imagery, visual edits, promotional creative, and story concepts.

03 / DISCOVERY

Google presence

Google Business Profile review, optimization opportunities, and monitoring of website clicks, directions, and bookings.

04 / NEIGHBORHOOD

Local relevance

Connecting restaurant content to neighborhood activity, brunch visits, seasonal dining occasions, and cultural moments.

05 / PROMOTIONS

Dining concepts

Prix fixe takeout presentation, dessert additions, cocktail storytelling, and World Pasta Day creative planning.

06 / PERFORMANCE

Evidence-led planning

Reviewing Instagram Insights and Google Business Profile trends to learn what attracts attention and what prompts customer action.

Scope note: This section brings together completed creative work, strategic planning, and optimization activities. Specific implementation evidence and outcome attribution should be added as items are confirmed with the restaurant.

06 / THE APPROACH

Creative decisions with a business purpose.

The guiding question is not merely “Does the post look good?” It is “Does this give someone a reason to discover, consider, or visit the restaurant?”

DISCOVER

Meet customers where they look.

Improve local visibility and build awareness beyond existing followers.

CONSIDER

Show a reason to visit.

Use real food, authentic restaurant moments, and relevant occasions to move people from browsing to interest.

ACT

Measure meaningful behavior.

Track taps, calls, direction requests, Google bookings, and eventually confirmed reservations and sales.
