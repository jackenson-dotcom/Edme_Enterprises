03 About Edme

Jackenson Edme
Runs Edme Enterprises LLC
New York City

Founded by Jackson Edme. Run by Jackenson Edme.

Edme Enterprises LLC provides marketing, customer acquisition, market research and business development support for founders launching a business and owners ready to grow.

Jackenson brings a hands-on approach to understanding a business, finding the right audience and turning strategy into campaigns. His experience spans restaurant marketing, local business strategy and international market-entry projects, alongside consumer research and brand positioning through an externship.

His background includes marketing studies focused on international business at Baruch College’s Zicklin School of Business, and a cross-cultural perspective as a native French speaker working in New York.
